### 🔮 공수명리학 · Gongsu Myeongri
A Saju (Four Pillars) personality test for BL fiction fans, made for fun.<br/>
<sub>생년월일로 보는 BL 공수 성향 풀이 (오락용 웹서비스)</sub>

- Enter a birth date and get a character type based on the Heavenly Stems, Earthly Branches and Five Elements, plus a "destiny partner" match and a short branching story
- Type codex, shareable result cards (Canvas + Web Share API), and result links you can send to friends
- Consent-based GA4 analytics with a privacy policy. Birth dates are never sent, and events follow the funnel (start → result → story → share)
- Shipped and improved through versioned releases (v1.x)

### 💼 월급루팡 서바이벌 · Salary Lupin Survival
A browser survival game about surviving the afternoon at the office.<br/>
<sub>퇴근 시간까지 살아남아라. 업무는 적당히, 멘탈은 지키고, 상사 의심도는 낮게.</sub>

- Balance three meters (work progress, mental health, boss suspicion) from 1 PM until you can leave
- 5 actions with time costs, a 3-second "Alt+Tab" dodge when the boss walks over, 17 random events, 7 endings
- A small exercise in game balancing, built in a single `index.html` with no libraries

### 🎸 박자 반전! 백스테이지 · Backstage Rhythm
A 30-second rhythm game where the rules flip halfway through.<br/>
<sub>공연 15초 전, 매니저가 외친다. "이제 반대로 연주하세요!"</sub>

- At 15 seconds the manager bursts in, and from then on you have to press the *opposite* arrow
- Music and sound effects are synthesized in Python, with no external audio files
- Judgment and scoring logic kept separate from rendering, with automated tests and audio-latency calibration
- Built for the web with pygbag, so you can play it in a browser without installing anything

### 👁️ Vision Lab & Reaction Cam
Hands-on computer vision notes from an AI course, plus a small browser app built from them.<br/>
<sub>YOLO26 · MediaPipe로 배우는 컴퓨터 비전 입문</sub>

- Object detection, segmentation and tracking with Ultralytics YOLO26 and OpenCV
- Hand landmarks, gesture recognition, and a face-landmark AR mustache filter with MediaPipe
- Custom gesture pipeline: collect hand landmarks in a Tkinter UI, train a scikit-learn classifier, then recognize the gestures in real time
- **Reaction Cam**: a browser version that uses MediaPipe Tasks to react to gestures and expressions, with all video processed on the device
